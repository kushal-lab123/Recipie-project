BARFI

INGREDIENTS

  • Milk Powder

    Sugar

    Ghee

    Milk

    Flavorings

    Garnish

    • PROCESS

  • Mix Ingredients: In a bowl, whisk together the milk powder, sugar, and milk until smooth.

    Cook: Transfer the mixture to a non-stick pan. Add ghee and cook on low flame, stirring constantly to prevent sticking.

    Thicken: Continue cooking for about 10–15 minutes until the mixture thickens, leaves the sides of the pan, and forms a dough-like consistency.

    Add Flavor: Stir in the cardamom powder.

    Set: Transfer to a greased tray or a tray lined with parchment paper. Spread evenly, garnish with pistachios, and press gently.

    Cool & Cut: Allow it to set for at least 1 hour (or up to 3 hours) in the refrigerator or at room temperature. Cut into squares and serve.

GULAB JAMUN

INGREDIENTS

  • Dough: 200g Khoya (mawa), 200g Paneer (freshly grated), 3-4 tbsp Maida (all-purpose flour), tsp Baking Powder, 1-2 tbsp Milk (if needed).

    Syrup: 1.5 cups Sugar, 1.5 cups Water, 4-5 Green Cardamoms (crushed), 1 tsp Rose water (optional), Saffron strands.

    Frying: Ghee or Oil for deep frying.

    • PROCESS

  • Combine milk powder, all-purpose flour, and baking soda in a bowl.

    Add ghee and rub into the dry mixture until crumbly.

    Add milk little by little to form a soft, smooth dough (do not knead). Cover and rest the dough for 10–15 minutes.

    Shape into small,crack-free round balls.Heat ghee or oil on low heat for frying.

    Heat ghee or oil on low heat for frying,Remove and drain on paper towel.

    Simultaneously, make sugar syrup by boiling sugar and water with cardamom until one-string consistency.

    Add rose water or lemon juice (optional).Soak the fried balls in warm syrup for at least 30 minutes.Serve warm or at room temperature.

KAJU KATLI

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 cup (approx. 200g) Cashew nuts (whole/broken)

    1/2 cup Sugar

    1 tsp Ghee

    1/4 cup Water

    1/4 tsp Cardamom powder (optional)

    Edible Silver Foil (Vark) - optional, for decoration

    • PROCESS

  • Powder Cashews: Pulse cashew nuts in a blender to make a fine powder. Do not over-blend, or they will release oil.

    Sugar Syrup: In a non-stick pan, add sugar and water.Bring to a boil until the sugar dissolves and it becomes slightly sticky (1-string consistency).

    Mix & Cook: Lower the heat.Add the cashew powder and cardamom powder to the syrup. Mix well until smooth.

    Thicken: Cook on low-medium heat, stirring continuously, until the mixture starts leaving the sides of the pan (about 4-5 minutes).

    Ghee:Add 1 tsp ghee to make it glossy.

    Knead:Take the dough out and let it cool slightly.Knead it until smooth.

    Roll: Place it between two greased butter papers and roll it to a 1/4cm thickness.

    Cut: Apply silver foil, let it cool completely, and cut into diamond shapes.
